Privacy & Data

Your data is stored locally in this browser.

Your data stays private

All data is stored locally and never sent to our servers.

Imported tours and GPS routes are stored only in the browser's IndexedDB.

No route or tour data is uploaded to TrailRoam servers.

Map place searches are sent to Photon (Komoot) to resolve names. Coordinate searches stay local.

Stored data can be inspected using browser developer tools.
